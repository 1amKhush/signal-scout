Pair a phone (optional)

The phone only adds location to your measurements. Wi-Fi is always measured by this laptop.

  1. Join the phone to the same Wi-Fi as this laptop and scan the code.
  2. The phone warns about the certificate (this laptop signs its own). Tap Advanced → Proceed.
  3. Tap Share location and allow precise location.
  4. Carry the phone with the laptop during the survey.

If the page never loads on the phone, the network probably blocks device-to-device traffic (common on campus and guest Wi-Fi). Everything else works without the phone.
